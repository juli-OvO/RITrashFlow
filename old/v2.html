1. The State of Things

Rhode Island's Waste at a Glance

Nearly 70% of what Rhode Islanders throw away ends up in the landfill. The state is falling far short of its own recycling mandates. 29.5% overall diversion rate statewide / 35% is the state-mandated minimum / only 15 of 39 municipalities meet it.

Rhode Island's Waste at a Glance: 29.5% statewide diversion, below the 35% mandate.

Where Your Trash Actually Goes: Johnston lands at 1.76 tons per household, while Portsmouth lands at 0.60.

What You Recycle Isn't Always Recycled: Providence reaches 648 pounds rejected per household.

The Gap Between Best and Worst: North Kingstown diverts 55.1%, while Providence diverts 7.8%.

The Clock Is Running: the Central Landfill is projected to reach capacity in 2046.
